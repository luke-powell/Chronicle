Biome and river overhaul — 2026-10-24

Research and design

Real vegetation responds to available water and temperature, not a biome ID.
Rainfall alone is insufficient: evaporation, seasonal drought, soils and
groundwater affect plant access to water. Mountains produce wetter windward
slopes and drier leeward areas. Riparian vegetation can therefore persist in
otherwise dry regions without changing the whole region into tropical forest.

Primary references consulted:
* NOAA/NWS climate of Hawaii: orographic rain and prevailing winds.
  https://www.weather.gov/hfo/climate_summary
* USGS watersheds: drainage area connects upstream precipitation to streamflow.
  https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/watersheds-and-drainage-basins
* US Forest Service: water needs of riparian and wetland vegetation.
  https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/41207
* Red Blob Games: separate elevation/moisture fields and multiscale noise.
  https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/terrain-from-noise/
* Red Blob Mapgen4: wind/rainfall, terrain and drainage as connected systems.
  https://www.redblobgames.com/maps/mapgen4/
* Barnes, Lehman and Mulla: Priority-Flood depression filling.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04463

These are design references, not copied implementations. This is a stylized
game ecology model; field values are normalized suitability, not measured
degrees Celsius or millimeters of rain.

Implementation

1. Keep the approved mountain geometry. Use independently rotated gradient
   noise for climate, with broad warped regions and weaker regional variation.
2. Cache 4096-unit weather nodes and smoothly interpolate them. Temperature,
   rain, seasonal drought and soil retention have separate spatial fields.
   Sample terrain upwind to approximate uplift and rain shadows. Cooling with
   elevation affects tree line, tropical suitability and snow.
3. Compute available moisture from rain, heat, seasonality and soil retention.
   Riverbanks add moisture according to distance and height above water.
   Combine contributions from all nearby reaches to avoid nearest-river seams.
4. Derive normalized overlapping meadow, woodland, tropical, wetland, dryland
   and alpine weights. Only music chooses a dominant category. Ground texture
   overlays, forest density and tree species use continuous suitability.
   Actual tree placement and distant canopy use the same density/species rules.
5. Collect rainfall-weighted runoff over twelve upstream graph steps. Breach
   shallow pits within three drainage cells to connect more tributaries;
   accept only strictly lower endpoints, with a maximum sampled cut of 110
   units. Preserve deeper enclosed basins. Channels widen toward downstream
   runoff and meander more on gentle slopes. All topology uses global doubles.
6. Bound caches and index nearby reaches by drainage cell. Coarse terrain
   footprints filter out subpixel riverbank detail instead of building distant
   hydrology. Existing tree population ownership and mesh LOD are unchanged.

Why not global Priority-Flood?

It is a useful reference for removing artificial terrain pits, but a single
whole-map flood needs a defined domain and outlets. Applying it independently
to streamed chunks would invent outlets at their borders. This implementation
instead uses bounded depression breaching with global node ownership, keeping
generation independent of chunk arrival order. It does not implement global
erosion, unrestricted watershed accumulation, sediment transport or seasonal
water simulation. Very large basins remain approximations. Rivers still use
the existing fine-mesh carving and water rendering pipeline.

Verification

tools/test_biomes.cpp checks normalized continuous ecology/species weights,
weather cell seams, riparian response, drainage topology, strictly downhill
water, protected original town, and coordinates through +/-1 trillion.
tools/test_forest_kernel.cpp checks identical tree populations when splitting
chunks and complementary LOD weights. tools/test_terrain_kernel.cpp checks
foundations, roads, surface collision, balanced LOD and edge continuity.
tools/preview_biomes.cpp exports the actual generator; render_ecology_maps.py
plots its fields. Real-game screenshots use isolated test saves.

Follow-up: mountain geometry, altitude ecology and terrain-aware channel routing
were subsequently overhauled; see mountain-generation-research.txt for current
behavior and validation.
