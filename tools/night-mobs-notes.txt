Norune night mob prototype

Uses the native Skeleton Soldier (e03a, HP 23), Dasher (e06a, HP 23)
and Cave Bat (e60a, HP 12) assets and idle/walk/death animations.

Spawn rules approximate single-player Minecraft hostile surface spawning:
- Darkness: the existing night phase; active outdoor torches exclude nearby spawns.
- 48 to 128 metres from Toan, using 10 retail units per metre.
- Global population cap 14, same-species packs of 1 to 4; four attempts per second.
  Both cap and attempt rate are 80% below the initial prototype.
- Dry, resident terrain, outside Georama building footprints; no terrain generation
  or streaming waits are requested by spawn checks.
- Immediate despawn beyond 128 metres; outside 32 metres, after 30 seconds,
  a 1/800 chance per 20 Hz decision tick.
- Detection within 32 metres with terrain/building line of sight, pursuit until
  48 metres, steering around steep steps, water and other enemies.
- Sword strikes use the existing town attack timing and equipped weapon attack.
- Daylight stops spawning and damages ALL three species by max HP / 8 per second.
  Integer remainders preserve the fractional damage: full HP reaches zero on the
  eighth tick. Death animation remains briefly before removal.

This is a continuous-terrain approximation, not a Minecraft engine port: torch
light is a radius rather than a voxel light field, attempts are globally budgeted,
and no cave/roof spawning is implemented. Simulation pauses in menus, interiors,
Georama and Dran flight. Population is transient, not serialized. Loot and weapon XP are not implemented.

Combat: attacks have wind-up, impact-time range/facing/obstruction checks and
recovery. Sword hits interrupt attacks with retail hit reactions and flashes.
Death clips finish before the corpse fades. Dedicated dungeon impacts use mixer
port 9 without replacing town sounds. Toan can guard; damage has 45 ticks of
invulnerability, HP display and hurt animation. Knockout returns to the arrival
position with full HP after 150 simulation ticks, with no currency penalty.
R3 toggles nearest visible target within 32 metres; lock drops on death, loss
of line of sight or distance beyond 48 metres. Camera and facing track target.
L3 now calls Dran / lands, replacing the previous R3 binding.

Reference:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/reference/content/spawnrulesreference/examples/spawnrulescomponents/spawn_rules

Validation:
- tools/test_night_spawn.cpp: pure distance, darkness, torch, water, cap,
  despawn and fractional sunlight arithmetic boundary tests.
- python tools/test_town_mobs.py: copied slot 1, native assets, all species,
  population cap, pursuit, travel despawn, dawn suppression and eight-second death.
  Offscreen captures and logs are under port/run/mod-tests/integration.
- python tools/test_town_mob_combat.py: actual HP loss, misses, interrupts,
  progressing hurt/death animations, R3 input and mixed impact audio.
- DC_MOBS_TRACE enables lifecycle diagnostics. world.mobs.load/tick/draw scopes
  participate in the existing opt-in CPU profiler.
- DC_TEST_MOBS_NIGHT, DC_TEST_MOBS_VIEW, DC_TEST_MOBS_CYCLE and DC_TEST_MOBS_SUN
  are automated-test controls only. Normal launches must omit them.
