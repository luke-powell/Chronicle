Terrain performance and player save follow-up, 2026-10-24

Causes and fixes
- Placing Georama cleared every cached chunk, triggering the blocking initial
  load path. Compare old/new foundation and road footprints instead; invalidate
  intersecting chunks including border/normal sampling margins. Removed and
  moved objects invalidate both their old and new footprints.
- Chunk CPU generation exceeded the frame budget because the budget was only
  checked BETWEEN builds. A single persistent worker now builds CPU payloads
  against immutable snapshots of the edit state. Hydrology caches stay warm on
  that worker. Vulkan uploads remain on the render thread and are spread across
  frames. Revision checks reject obsolete placement results.
- Keep the previous complete selection visible while replacement terrain,
  canopy, and all tree LOD meshes are prepared. No new foliage range cutoff,
  reduced population, or camera-dependent eviction was introduced.
- Atmospheric integration uses 4..16 midpoint samples according to variation
  across aerosol scale heights instead of always spending 16 samples on every
  nearby tree vertex. Physical coefficients and the visible horizon are unchanged.

Measured with actual game data at 1280x720
- Original camera/zoom trace: maximum ordinary terrain update 22.410 ms.
- Worker trace: maximum ordinary terrain update 1.952 ms.
- Final far-world camera/zoom trace with staged uploads: p99 terrain update
  1.302 ms, maximum 2.739 ms. These are terrain update costs, NOT total frame times.
- Placement regression invalidated 48 of 1828 chunks rather than clearing all.
- Initial cold load and deliberate far teleports remain blocking and expensive
  (roughly 47..60 seconds in these tests). This work does not promise zero FPS
  drops or instantaneous first-time landing.

Save restoration
- world.json now stores optional town, double x/y/z, and player yaw alongside
  existing precision placements, tied to save.dat by the existing fingerprint.
- Old saves remain readable. Invalid player data is ignored; other scenes keep
  native spawn behavior. Actual town saves capture the player, not the editor cursor.
- Restoration rebases the scene and camera, clears motion, and survives the
  retail arrival event overwriting its initial placement. Later terrain edits
  may raise the player to the ground to prevent an underground spawn.
- User-requested legacy recovery: latest numbered save 3; Toan house at
  (56600,-55450), Macho house at (-345700,644800). These are separate sites.
  Verified safe standing location beside Macho: (-345620,1431.2012939453125,644800).
  Back up original world.json before adding the recovery player entry.
