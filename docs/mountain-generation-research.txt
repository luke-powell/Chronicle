Mountain and elevation overhaul — 2026-10-24

Findings

The previous kernel added ridged noise at every mountain scale. It lacked a
separate valley-floor shape and local upland shelves. More importantly, the
renderer forced rock coverage to 100% above 3,100 game units, regardless of
slope. Relatively gentle terrain therefore still looked like bare mountainside.
The original high-altitude snow override also covered much of the mountain
before there was space for woodland, subalpine meadow and exposed upper rock.

Primary research

USGS, Geology of Glacier National Park:
https://www.usgs.gov/geology-and-ecology-of-national-parks/geology-glacier-national-park
Glacial erosion produces broad-bottomed valleys with steep walls, hanging
valleys, cirques and sharp ridges. This motivated separating floor and wall
profiles rather than uniformly increasing or decreasing roughness.

NPS, Yosemite meadows:
https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/meadows.htm
Meadows can occupy relatively flat landforms surrounded by steep terrain;
water availability and soils matter alongside altitude. This motivated keeping
soil-covered uplands and integrating the existing continuous climate fields.

NPS, Yosemite geology exhibits:
https://home.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/geology-exhibits.htm
Range-scale uplift, asymmetric slopes, erosion and rockfall create different
landforms within one mountain landscape; talus accumulates below cliffs.

Genevaux et al., Terrain Modelling from Feature Primitives (2015):
https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/bbenes/papers/Genevaux15CGF.pdf
Terrain features can be represented and combined explicitly instead of being
left entirely to generic noise. This implementation uses analytic profiles;
it does not copy the paper's feature-tree implementation.

Grenier et al., Real-time Terrain Enhancement with Controlled Procedural
Patterns (2024):
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cgf.14992
Procedural detail should respond to the larger terrain structure. Here, rough
detail is reduced on shelves and valley floors and retained on shoulders.

Ian Parberry, Designer Worlds (2014):
https://jcgt.org/published/0003/01/04/paper.pdf
Combining geographic structure with local detail is valuable for infinite
terrain. No external elevation datasets or proprietary terrain assets are used.

Implementation

MountainAt separates a broad range envelope, multiscale crests/spurs, warped
valley corridors, gently rolling elevated floors, local shelves and crag detail.
Horizontal mountain wavelengths increased by 25% while peak relief remains
comparable. Town height is unchanged within the protected starting area.

An early repeated-stratum experiment made obvious contour terraces; it was
discarded. A smoothly varying local shelf elevation now compresses only a
limited band on selected mountainsides. There is no repeating stair-step
quantization or abrupt elevation discontinuity.

RockExposure uses slope plus broken upper-alpine terrain, replacing the blanket
altitude mask. The original ground, rock and icebig textures remain in use.
Rock and snow use triplanar texture sampling, preventing the horizontal ground
projection from stretching into vertical stripes on cliff faces.
Treeline and snowline respond to temperature and now leave a larger vertical
interval for forest, meadow and exposed rock before summit snow. Nearby trees
and distant canopy share a slope suitability function so cliffs stay exposed.

The natural-height cache is fixed-size and keyed by exact global double inputs
and sample spacing. It caches only immutable natural terrain, never town
foundations or roads. This avoids repeating terrain work across triangle,
collision and vegetation sampling without caching stale editor changes.

Validation

tools/preview_mountains.cpp exports the actual kernel, including geometric
slope and surface/ecology suitability. tools/render_mountain_maps.py compares
matching regions and scales. In the sampled 180,000-unit region, the peak stays
about 26,100 units. Roughly 37% of sampled uplands remain below 15 degrees,
while steep terrain is concentrated in walls and escarpments. Soil-capable uplands
replace the former effectively universal rock coverage.

tools/test_mountains.cpp checks scale, coexistence of gentle terrain/cliffs,
valleys/shelves, slope-based material rules, starting-town preservation, and
continuity through trillion-unit coordinates. Existing terrain, foundation,
river/climate and exact forest population tests are also run. Real-game views
use isolated saves and the production renderer.

Scope

This is an analytic approximation inspired by geomorphology, not a geological
time simulation. It does not simulate glaciers, sediment mass balance, talus
particles or tectonic plates. Steep rock is terrain geometry/material, not new
scattered boulder models. Drainage is rebuilt on the new landform surface. Each candidate connection
first tries a terrain-following profile, then a bounded 256-unit A* corridor.
A state carries water elevation; a successor cannot increase it or pass through
a lower hollow and subsequently climb back out. Cuts are limited to 128 units.
Segments are checked against terrain at intervals no larger than 64 units.
Confluence curves are accepted only if they satisfy the same checks; steep
reaches narrow according to terrain slope. Riverbank wetness scales with channel
width, avoiding equally wide green corridors around tiny headwaters.

The new river regression independently samples at 32-unit intervals. Across
158 active non-lake reaches and 4,000 segments it checks descending water,
endpoint connections, no airborne centerline water and no deep ridge shortcuts,
including coordinates around one trillion. This is a bounded catchment model,
not an unrestricted hydrological solve; broad closed basins remain approximate.

Saved building foundations remain authoritative, while
unmodified procedural terrain regenerates with the new elevation model.

Performance: cold initial terrain preparation measured about 48 seconds in the
final pre-material test, down from about 56 seconds before reducing unnecessary
climate samples on coarse horizon canopy tiles. Full cold relocation tests
still take substantially longer than steady-state play; this is not a claim of
instant loading or a fixed frame-rate guarantee. Tree mesh populations and
canopy coverage remain present at all existing levels.
