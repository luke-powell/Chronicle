Terrain performance and player save follow-up, 2026-10-24

Causes and fixes
- Placing Georama cleared every cached chunk, triggering the blocking initial
  load path. Compare old/new foundation and road footprints instead; invalidate
  intersecting chunks including border/normal sampling margins. Removed and
  moved objects invalidate both their old and new footprints.
- Chunk CPU generation exceeded the frame budget because the budget was only
  checked BETWEEN builds. A single persistent worker now builds CPU payloads
  against immutable snapshots of the edit state. Hydrology caches stay warm on
  that worker. Vulkan uploads remain on the render thread and are spread across
  frames. Revision checks reject obsolete placement results.
- Keep the previous complete selection visible while replacement terrain,
  canopy, and all tree LOD meshes are prepared. No new foliage range cutoff,
  reduced population, or camera-dependent eviction was introduced.
- Atmospheric integration uses 4..16 midpoint samples according to variation
  across aerosol scale heights instead of always spending 16 samples on every
  nearby tree vertex. Physical coefficients and the visible horizon are unchanged.

Measured with actual game data at 1280x720
- Original camera/zoom trace: maximum ordinary terrain update 22.410 ms.
- Worker trace: maximum ordinary terrain update 1.952 ms.
- Final far-world camera/zoom trace with staged uploads: p99 terrain update
  1.302 ms, maximum 2.739 ms. These are terrain update costs, NOT total frame times.
- Placement regression invalidated 48 of 1828 chunks rather than clearing all.
- Initial cold load and deliberate far teleports remain blocking and expensive
  (roughly 47..60 seconds in these tests). This work does not promise zero FPS
  drops or instantaneous first-time landing.

Save restoration
- world.json now stores optional town, double x/y/z, and player yaw alongside
  existing precision placements, tied to save.dat by the existing fingerprint.
- Old saves remain readable. Invalid player data is ignored; other scenes keep
  native spawn behavior. Actual town saves capture the player, not the editor cursor.
- Restoration rebases the scene and camera, clears motion, and survives the
  retail arrival event overwriting its initial placement. Later terrain edits
  may raise the player to the ground to prevent an underground spawn.
- User-requested legacy recovery: latest numbered save 3; Toan house at
  (56600,-55450), Macho house at (-345700,644800). These are separate sites.
  Verified safe standing location beside Macho: (-345620,1431.2012939453125,644800).
  Back up original world.json before adding the recovery player entry.

Regression tools
- tools/test_terrain_streaming.py: local invalidation, single initial load,
  retained initial canopy across all levels, and ordinary update p99 budget.
- tools/test_world_player_save.py: real native autosave, sidecar and exact global

2026-10-24: shared player-centred resolution rings
------------------------------------------------
Selection is now an 8x8 finest square followed by 18 nested 8x8 squares
with their 4x4 interiors removed. Boundaries snap to two tiles at each
level. This gives exactly 928 terrain tiles, each 16x16 cells / 512
triangles (475,136 base terrain triangles total). Water, ground materials,
snow and foliage are additional passes, not included in that base count.
Tile width doubles at each level. The finest square guarantees a 1024-unit
radius in X/Z around the tracked point. The outer square guarantees over
2,300 miles in every direction, and remains centred on that tracked point.
Walking/flight track the player; Georama tracks the placement cursor.
Camera altitude and orientation never remove the finest ring.

Local asynchronous publication pairs near refinements with trailing coarsenings,
so its rendered tile count stays fixed while movement shifts resolution.
The old complete coverage remains displayed until replacement chunks
(including foliage) have uploaded. Pending/cache meshes consume extra
bounded storage; they are not extra rendered rings. Teleports still require
loading: a finite renderer cannot promise instant generation of unseen data.

Foliage representation is owned by each terrain tile: native model LODs
at levels 0..2, native-texture instanced billboards at 3..7, aggregate
forest coverage on the ground mesh beyond that. Billboard physical sizes
are independent of tile size. There are no camera-distance visibility fades
for these ring-owned trees. Aggregate canopy is no longer raised 150 units
over the land. Far aggregate coverage is ecological density sampling, not
an exhaustive enumeration of trillions of individual trees. Individual
foliage counts still vary by biome; only the base terrain triangle count
is identical between tiles.

Validation: test_terrain_rings.cpp checks exact budgets, near resolution,
coverage and balanced seams across 240 positions including +/-1e12, and
incremental publications. test_ring_views.py checks zooming out/in
retains 928 tiles and finest LOD. test_world_streaming.py also checks the
player's resident tile remains LOD 0 throughout its sustained flight.

Editor retargeting and landing regression
-----------------------------------------
- Landing probes now share a fixed starting altitude. Previously each successful
  probe lowered the next ray to one unit above the previous hit; an uphill next
  sample could lie above that ray. Reproduced at world (10000, 289818): the fourth
  probe missed before the change and hits afterwards.
- Editor target changes discard the previous local publication plan. Finished
  results no longer needed by the new editor target are discarded before upload.
- Upload ordering first completes dependencies of the current local publication,
  then orders by distance to the current focus. Four workers remain available
  for near geometry during editor panning, even during coarse local refinement.
- Large jumps skip expensive old/new region connectivity construction; the near
  refinement path and complete-target publication handle those transitions.
- Far aerial cursor height samples the resident terrain triangles. Holding a
  building, zooming close and landing retain exact height queries. This avoids
  synchronous detailed world generation solely to move a distant camera target.
- tools/test_editor_retarget.py drives actual editor controls against copied saves
  for both the slope case and zoomed-out pan/reversal/zoom-in/landing.
- Unselected aerial cursor drawing reuses the resident surface sample instead of
  generating exact terrain twice more during drawing. Retained foliage uses the
  new editor target's LOD while replacement tiles are prepared.
- Road drape caches use authoritative world positions and stable part identity.
  Floating-origin shifts no longer invalidate their meshes or road foundations;
  actual placement/foundation edits still invalidate the cache. Profiling scopes
  world.georama.road_drape and world.georama.road_build expose this cost.
- Validation: slope exit, rapid pan/reversal/zoom/exit and sustained flight pass.
  The copied-save editor traversal builds 25 road meshes initially and zero after
  travel begins, with no terrain edit invalidations caused by camera movement.
